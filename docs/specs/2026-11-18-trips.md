# Trips (reys) when every truck is full

Status: built 2026-11-18.

## Problem

The route split the day's load between the trucks: one if it fits, two if not. When
even both trucks together could not take it, the page said «Sig'maydi … kg ortiqcha»
and planned nothing. In practice the trucks come back and load again: a second reys.

## Behaviour

- One or two trucks still go as before (`_plan_one_trip`): fill one first, or two in
  parallel with «Tezroq tugatish».
- When that is not enough, `routing.plan_trips` plans trips:
  - Every active truck goes on the 1-reys, including a third one if there is one.
  - Trucks that are back load again for a 2-reys, bigger first. The planner uses the
    fewest trips that hold the load, up to `MAX_TRIPS` (4) per truck.
  - Each trip takes one stretch of the city. This is the same sweep by direction from
    the warehouse that `split_two` uses, generalised to N slots (`fill_trips`). The
    fallback packs the heaviest stops first.
  - Routes come trip by trip, so a truck's 1-reys comes before its 2-reys.
    `routing.trip_numbers` derives the reys number from the truck order.
- Past `MAX_TRIPS` the day is still `OverCapacity`. A stop heavier than the biggest
  truck is still `StopTooHeavy`.

## Route page

- Each reys has its own card, «A — 2-reys», keeping the truck's colour. The driver's
  message title says the reys too.
- The summary reads «A (2 reys) + B — hammasi bir qatnovda sig'madi — 2 reys».
- A 2-reys leaves when the truck is back from the 1-reys, plus
  `ETA_RELOAD_MINUTES` (30) for loading. Its times are still counted in minutes from
  the day's departure, so the live clock moves them together with the 1-reys.
- ⇄ cycles a stop through every card. Saving accepts any number of routes
  (`split_routes = N`).
- «Jo'natish» sets only the 1-reys stops to «Yo'lda». The 2-reys stops stay
  «Rejada»/«Ortilmoqda», because their goods are still in the warehouse.
- The money car is not offered on a day with trips. Its cost model treats every route
  as a vehicle out at the same time, which a truck's later reys is not.

## Known limits

- **The trips aren't stored, only worked out.** The route is planned from the stops
  not yet delivered. Usually the load is just over what the trucks hold, so as soon
  as the first stop is delivered the rest fit in one go. The 2-reys card then merges
  back into the 1-reys cards, well before the 1-reys is finished. The plan is right
  the evening before and in the morning, which is when it is loaded and sent.
  - When a truck leaves on its 2-reys, set those stops to «Yo'lda» with their own
    status box on the day board.
  - Don't re-post «Jo'nash vaqti» for it: by then the plan has merged, so that would
    send every remaining stop and overwrite the morning's departure.
  - The fix is a follow-up: store each stop's reys (a field and a migration) when the
    trucks leave, plus a «Jo'natish» per reys.
- On the map a truck's reys share its colour. The 2-reys pins read «2.1, 2.2…».
- The reload time (30 min) is a guess until the client gives a figure.

# Money car (pul olish mashinasi)

Client request, 2026-11-13: when there are many money-only stops, a light car
should collect the money on its own route, the opposite way from the truck, so
the truck gets done sooner.

## Decisions

All five were settled with the client on 2026-11-13.

1. **No payment recording.** It's a route only; money is entered as before.
2. **The system decides, like it decides the trucks.** When the car makes the
   day cheaper, the suggested route simply has it, with a «Tizim tavsiyasi» note
   saying why. No button and no save needed. «Pul mashinasisiz» turns it off
   for the day (client, 2026-11-14).
3. **The car leaves from the warehouse.** It gets the same Google/Yandex/Telegram
   links as a truck.
4. **A money-only stop can stay on a truck.** The optimizer decides; there's no
   fixed rule.
5. **Scoring: time and km, with km weighted more, and a fixed cost for the car.**
   `cost = minutes until the last vehicle is back + KM_WEIGHT × km driven
   + MONEY_CAR_FIXED_COST if the car goes`.
   - `KM_WEIGHT = 2`: 1 km counts as 2 minutes.
   - Time per route is km / 40 km/h plus 15 min per stop, the same figures as
     the arrival times.
   - `MONEY_CAR_FIXED_COST = 60`. Every stop costs a truck 15 minutes even
     right beside a delivery, so without this a few on-the-way money stops
     would already call the car. With it, the car is offered only when the
     time and km it saves outweigh the 60.
   - The button shows when the day with the car is cheaper, fixed cost
     included.

## Algorithm (`crm/routing.py`, `plan_money_car`)

- **What may move.** Delivery stops (`deliver`, `both`) keep their truck. Only
  money-only stops (`collect`) may move: to another truck, or to the car.
- **The search.** Local search with relocate moves (a stop to the cheapest place
  on another vehicle, taken while the day's cost falls). Every route is then
  tidied with 2-opt + or-opt (runs of 1–3 stops), repeated until nothing improves.
- **Starting points.** The trucks as planned; every money stop on the car; and
  cheapest-insertion placements, far-first and near-first. The best result wins.
- **The comparison.** The same search without the car gives the baseline, so the
  car is only credited with what it alone saves.
- **"Opposite direction".** No separate rule. For time and km a loop costs the
  same either way. The car naturally ends up with the side of the city the
  trucks aren't working.
- **Speed.** About 15 ms for 45 stops, so it runs on every route view.

## Storage and page

- **`LoadItem.money_car`** records which stops ride the car (and `position` their
  order), so the day board numbers them P1, P2….
  - **Unsaved order (the suggestion):** the route view decides afresh each time
    and records its choice. Nothing to press.
  - **Saved order («Tartibni saqlash»):** the car card's stops are posted as
    `car` and saved with the trucks' order.
  - **Moving a stop to another day** clears its flag. The flag only counts while
    the stop is money-only (`rides_money_car`).
- **`Load.money_car_off`** («Pul mashinasisiz»): the day goes without the car.
  The banner then says what the car would save, with «Pul mashinasini yoqish».
- **A saved order without the car** is not overridden. The banner recommends the
  car with «Qo'llash», which re-runs the plan (a stale page changes nothing) and
  saves it.
- **«Tavsiya etilgan tartib»** goes back to the suggestion, so the car is decided
  afresh.
- **The car's card** has its own colour (`--warning`): stops with debt, arrival
  times and share links. Its order is the system's, with no ↑ ↓ or ⇄.
